Statement of Faith

  1. We are Cross Point: Our teachings are rooted in the transformative power of the cross.
  2. The Trinity: We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons - Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  3. Jesus' Death and Resurrection: We believe Jesus Christ died for our sins, was buried, and rose again on the third day, securing our salvation.
  4. Baptism: We believe in water baptism as a symbol of new life in Christ.
  5. Spiritual Gifts: We believe in the manifestation of spiritual gifts, including speaking in tongues, as described in Scripture.
  6. Stewardship: We believe in tithing and generous giving as an act of worship and obedience to God.

We unite around these core beliefs and welcome all who share them. While we respect differing perspectives, our church's teachings and practices will remain grounded in these fundamental doctrines.
